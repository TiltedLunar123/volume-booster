VOLUME BOOSTER - YOUTUBE THUMBNAIL
Target: 1280x720, 16:9, under 2 MB, JPG or PNG.
Generate the art WITHOUT text, then composite the overlay hook yourself.

=====================================================================
OVERLAY HOOK (composite on the LEFT negative space, not baked by the model)
=====================================================================

    MAKE IT
    LOUDER

Three words, two lines. Heavy condensed sans (Anton, Archivo Black, or
Bahnschrift Bold). "MAKE IT" in pure white, "LOUDER" in amber #F59E0B.
All caps, tight leading, sitting in the lower-left third. Add a soft dark
drop shadow so it holds up against the background.

Alternative hook if you want the number instead, same treatment:
    600%
    LOUDER
("600%" in amber, "LOUDER" in white.)

=====================================================================
PROMPT
=====================================================================

Flat minimalist vector illustration, bold iconic graphic design, no text.
A single chunky horizontal volume slider running across the right half of
the frame, drawn as a thick rounded bar. The filled portion of the bar
runs a clean left-to-right colour ramp from vivid green through amber into
a hot red at the far right, and the round slider knob sits near the red
end, pushed hard past the top of the range. Above the bar, five simple
rounded level-meter bars step upward in height from left to right, the
short ones green, the tallest one red, like a meter pinned into the red
zone. Everything sits on a near-black charcoal background with a very
subtle dark green glow radiating from behind the slider.

Style: flat 2D vector, thick confident shapes, hard edges, high contrast,
generous clean negative space filling the entire LEFT half of the frame
for text to be added later. Simple, bold, iconic, poster-like. Crisp and
readable when shrunk to a small thumbnail. Perfectly executed, clean
geometry, no clutter.

Palette, use only these:
    background   #0E1013  near-black charcoal
    green        #22C55E
    amber        #F59E0B
    red          #EF4444
    white        #FFFFFF
    muted grey   #2B2F36  for the empty part of the slider track

Composition: left 50 percent almost entirely empty background, right 50
percent holds the slider and meter bars. Nothing important in the lower
left corner where the YouTube duration badge sits.

=====================================================================
NOTES
=====================================================================

- No blue, purple or cyan anywhere. The product's whole visual identity is
  the green-to-red level meter, and blue is what every other extension in
  this category uses.
- Ask for no baked text. Models still get letterforms subtly wrong, and
  the hook needs to be sharp at small sizes.
- The colour ramp is doing real work: it is the same ramp as the extension's
  slider, badge and icon, so the thumbnail matches the product on sight.
- If the first result feels empty, ask for the meter bars to be larger
  rather than adding more elements. Keep the left half clear.
